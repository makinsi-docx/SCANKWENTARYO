01 / YOUR CATALOG

All your products, together.

Keep product names, SKUs, categories, unit costs, and storage locations easy to find. Search the catalog or filter it by category.

02 / STOCK MOVEMENT

Record stock in and sales.

Add incoming units or subtract items when they sell. Stock counts and inventory totals update with each adjustment.

A NOTE ABOUT YOUR DATA

Saved in this browser, not in the cloud.

Scankwentory stores inventory and local account details in your browser on this device. Accounts on the same browser share the inventory. This is a simple local tool—not a cloud service or server-backed security system. Export a CSV copy if you need a separate backup.
